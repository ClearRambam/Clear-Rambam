    Why this works

    Clarity comes from three things: steady breath, a loose jaw, and active lips and tongue tip. The routine warms each up in that order, then puts it together on real sentences. Ten minutes a day beats an hour once a week.

    Read aloud

    Read a passage the way you'd deliver it to a room. The app checks clarity and your speaking pace (aim for about 130–160 words per minute in a talk).
